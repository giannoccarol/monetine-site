## 1. What Monetine is

Monetine helps you record spending, income, account balances, loans and recurring payments, and shows you summaries and estimates. It is free and has no accounts. Your data stays on your phone.

## 2. Not financial advice

Monetine is not a financial, investment, tax or legal advisory service. Everything the app shows is an indicative estimate based on the data you entered, including:

- how much you can spend per day until the end of the month;
- loan schedules, early repayment and refinancing simulations, and loan cost assessments. The comparison with the Bank of Italy reference rates (TEGM) uses a table we update by hand every quarter and may be out of date;
- the AI assistant's answers, analyses and tips;
- personal inflation.

Decisions about your money are yours. Before important choices, check the figures with your bank, lender or a qualified advisor.

## 3. AI can make mistakes

When you use the AI features, you are interacting with an AI model, not a person. The model can misunderstand what you said, pick the wrong amount, date, category or account, invent figures or give inaccurate answers. When you do not say an amount, it estimates one from average prices and marks it as estimated.

- Check every proposal before confirming it. With confirmation on (the default), nothing is saved until you confirm. Deleting accounts and transactions always asks for confirmation.
- Every transaction can be edited or deleted afterwards.
- You are responsible for the data you save.

To use the AI features you connect a provider of your choice with your own key, or your own endpoint.

- Your relationship with the provider is direct and governed by its terms and privacy policy, including any age requirements. We are not a party to it.
- **The provider's costs are yours.** Providers usually charge by usage. Every message, analysis and import makes one or more requests; long conversations and large imports cost more. We do not control, receive or refund these charges. Set a spending limit with your provider.
- Keep your key safe. Monetine stores it encrypted on your phone and leaves it out of backups.
- If the provider is unavailable, changes its models or rejects requests, the AI features may stop working. The rest of the app keeps working.

## 5. Your data and backups are your responsibility

Your data exists only on your phone. We have no copy and cannot recover it.

- If you lose or reset your phone without a backup, the data is lost. Keep Android backup on, or make regular manual backups in the app's import and export section.
- Manual backup and CSV files are not encrypted: keep them somewhere safe.
- Restoring a backup replaces all current data.
- Check imported transactions before confirming. You can undo an import, which deletes its transactions.
