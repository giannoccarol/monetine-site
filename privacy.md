# Monetine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Monetine is an Android app for keeping track of spending, income and net worth. It is developed by Lorenzo Giannoccaro ("we"). This page explains which data the app uses, where it goes and how you control it.

## In short

- Your data stays on your phone. Monetine has no servers of its own, does not ask you to create an account and sends nothing to us.
- There are no ads, no usage statistics, no analytics and no tracking tools.
- If you connect an artificial intelligence (AI) provider, Monetine sends it the text you dictate or type and a summary of your finances, only when you use an AI feature. You choose the provider and use your own key.
- Speech recognition is Android's system service.
- If Android backup is on, a copy of the data goes to your Google account.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's storage on your phone:

- transactions (amount, date, title, category, merchant, notes, account);
- accounts with their monthly balances, categories, recurring transactions, loans and past imports;
- app settings (theme, reminder, chosen provider and model, custom endpoint address, amount to set aside);
- your AI provider keys, encrypted with an Android system key (Android Keystore) that cannot leave the phone.

We have no access to any of this data.

## What goes to the AI provider, and when

AI features are optional. Until you set up a provider in the app settings, Monetine sends nothing to anyone. You can always enter and browse your data by hand.

You pick the provider from the list (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral or OpenRouter) or enter your own endpoint, such as a model running on your computer. Monetine connects directly to the provider with your key: the data does not pass through us.

**Chat and dictation.** When you send a message to the assistant, typed or dictated, Monetine sends:

- your message and the earlier messages of the same conversation, including the assistant's proposals;
- a summary of your data, needed to answer: categories, accounts with bank and latest balance, the last 40 transactions (date, amount, title, category, merchant, account), totals per category for the last six months, recurring expenses, how much you can still spend this month, prices of repeat purchases over the last year, and loans (name, lender, amounts, rates, instalments).

The provider test in the settings sends the same data with a sample sentence.

**Analysis in Statistics.** Only when you ask for it, Monetine sends totals per category month by month, recurring expenses, prices of repeat purchases and the list of categories.

**Importing a bank statement.** If a provider is set up:

- for the rows the app's own rules cannot classify, it sends the description (up to 140 characters), counterparty, amount, type and the category written in the file, to get a suggested category and title;
- if the app does not recognise the file's columns, it sends the first 20 rows (each cell cut to 40 characters) so the model can work out the layout. These rows may include the header the bank puts at the top of the file, such as account holder and IBAN.

**Technical data.** As with any internet connection, the provider sees your phone's IP address. Monetine identifies itself as "Monetine/version (Android)". With OpenRouter it also sends the app's name; with OpenCode, a random code for each conversation.

**What the provider does with it.** Retention, use and location of the data depend on the terms of the provider you chose, with whom you have a direct relationship. Some providers, especially on free plans, may keep messages or use them to improve their models. Some are based outside the European Union, for example in the United States or China. Read the provider's privacy policy before connecting it. With a local model, the data stays on your network.

**Encryption.** The providers in the list use encrypted connections (HTTPS). For a custom endpoint, the address you type applies: with `http://` traffic is not encrypted, so use it only on your home network.

**AI-generated content.** The assistant's replies and proposals are generated by an AI model and may contain mistakes. No operation proposed by the assistant is saved without your confirmation, unless you turn off confirmation in the settings; deleting accounts and transactions always asks for confirmation.

## Speech recognition

To dictate, Monetine uses Android's system speech recognition service, the one set on your phone (usually Google's). Monetine asks for microphone permission but does not record or store audio: it only receives the recognised text. Depending on the phone and its settings, the system service may process your voice on the phone or on its provider's servers, under that provider's terms.

## Backup

**Android automatic backup.** Monetine allows Android backup. If it is on, Android copies the app's data (the database with transactions, accounts and balances, and the settings) to your Google account on Google Drive, and restores it on a new phone when you transfer your data. AI provider keys are excluded. The backup is run by Google under its terms and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android's backup settings.

**Manual backup and export.** In the app's import and export section you can create a full backup (a JSON file with all your data, without AI keys) or a CSV file of your transactions. You save them wherever you choose. The files are not encrypted: keep them safe. Restoring a backup replaces all current data.

## Widgets, notifications and sharing

- Home screen widgets show amounts, balances, titles and categories of transactions, visible to anyone who looks at your phone. With the option to hide amounts in widgets, amounts become "•••"; charts, percentages and titles remain.
- The evening reminder is off until you turn it on, and it shows no amounts.
- "Share" on the personal inflation card opens Android's share menu with a short text of percentages and prices. It only happens when you tap it, towards the app you pick.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** to dictate. Asked the first time you tap the microphone.
- **Notifications:** only for the evening reminder, when you turn it on.
- **Internet:** only to reach the AI provider you set up.
- **Run at startup and vibration:** to reschedule the reminder after a restart, and for haptic feedback.

## How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete single transactions, accounts and loans, undo an import, or erase everything by uninstalling the app or with "Clear storage" in Android settings. The Google Drive backup follows Android backup rules. For data already sent to an AI provider, contact the provider.

## Your rights

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you the right to access, correct and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a machine-readable format (portability). Since we do not hold your data, you exercise most of these rights directly in the app: you can view, edit, export and delete everything. For data sent to an AI provider, write to the provider.

For any question about this policy, write to lorenzo.giannoccaro998@gmail.com. If you live in the EU, you can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).
